GLOBAL HYDROGEN PRODUCTION TECHNOLOGIES

HyPT

The Global Hydrogen Production Technologies Center (HyPT) is a £14.1 million, five-year international research programme accelerating low-cost, net-zero hydrogen production and deployment at scale. It brings together researchers across the UK, US, Canada, Australia, Germany and Egypt, combining expertise in hydrogen production, materials, engineering and technology development. HyPT focuses on water electrolysis, methane pyrolysis and photocatalytic solar water splitting, with the goal of reducing hydrogen production costs and improving sustainability.

Budget UoB: 1.1M£

PROJECT

HiDroConnect

HiDroConnect (High-Durability Coatings for Solid Oxide Electrolyser Interconnects) develops durable protective coatings for solid oxide electrolyser interconnects, targeting reduced degradation and improved stack performance.

Budget: £350k

PROJECT

flexell

flexell (Flexible and Reversible Solid Oxide Cells) develops advanced solid oxide cell technologies for flexible operation with alternative fuels under both fuel cell and electrolyser modes, supporting efficient and adaptable low-carbon energy conversion.

Budget: €240k
